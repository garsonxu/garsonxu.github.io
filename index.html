大公司里，AI 战略推不动，往往不是技术不够，而是没人能同时跟董事会和机器对话。我做了 15 年这件事：从 FP&A 到 4 大洲 13 国的 ERP 实施，到数据中台与业务中台架构，到在一家十万人规模的跨国药企里，把 Agentic AI 从原型推到生产。懂业务的怕代码，懂代码的不懂业务，而我站在两者中间做翻译。

现任罗氏（中国）Digital & AI Solution Architect，主导研发部门中国区的 AI 战略落地，带领多个 Agentic AI 平台从 0 到 1 交付。2023-2025 连续三年获 Roche Valuable Contribution。我也写东西、办社区——一个二百多人的内部开源社区，和一个叫「碳硅之辨」的公众号系列，都是想把 AI 这件事讲给不写代码的人听。

In a large company, AI strategy stalls not because the technology is missing, but because no one can talk to the boardroom and to the machine at the same time. I have done exactly this for 15 years: from FP&A, to ERP rollouts across 13 countries on 4 continents, to data and business platform architecture, to pushing Agentic AI from prototype to production inside a hundred-thousand-person multinational pharma. Business people fear the code; engineers miss the business — I stand in between and translate.

Now Digital & AI Solution Architect at Roche (China), leading the China R&D AI strategy and the 0-to-1 delivery of several Agentic AI platforms. Roche Valuable Contribution three years running, 2023-2025. I also write and build community — a 200-plus-member internal open-source community, and a public essay series on the carbon-silicon question — both attempts to explain AI to the people who don't write code.

✦ 方法论与影响力 method & reach ✦
大型组织的 AI Native 转型，是一场自上而下和自下而上的双向奔赴。
AI-native transformation in a large org is a meeting halfway — top-down conviction and bottom-up momentum, each reaching for the other.

自上而下，是董事会真金白银的 token 预算与战略意志；自下而上，是工程师「好逸恶劳」的本能——每天琢磨怎么用 AI 偷懒。只有上面的意志找到了下面的场景，转型才真的发生。我的工作，是把这两端接上。

Top-down is the board's token budget and strategic will. Bottom-up is the engineer's instinct to automate every tedious thing in sight. Transformation happens only when the will from above finds its scenarios below — and my job is to wire the two together.

我发起的内部开源社区「硅基世界漫游指南」，把 AI 从代码开发扩展到业务侧偷懒——如今 200+ 人，出现了「人传人现象」。有人把每周数小时的台账自动化到几分钟，有人派 agent 替自己监听消息、建工单、上强制培训。

The internal open-source community I founded — "The Silicon Hitchhiker's Guide" — carried AI beyond coding into the business side's everyday drudgery. It now runs 200+ members and spreads person to person. People have collapsed hours of weekly bookkeeping into minutes, and sent agents to watch their messages, file tickets, and sit through mandatory training.
