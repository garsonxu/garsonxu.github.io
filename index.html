当前AI时代的主要矛盾，是人民日益增长的技术焦虑与贫乏的落地场景之间的矛盾。我做了15年的技术落地：从FP&A到4大洲13国的ERP实施，到数据中台与业务中台架构，到在一家十万人规模的跨国药企里把Agentic AI从原型推到生产，推动产业和政府端在临床与真实世界科研、医保综评等领域的打通与深入合作。我认为，不懂代码的财务不是好政府事务。

现任罗氏（中国）数字化与人工智能解决方案架构师，主导研发部门中国区的AI战略落地，带领多个Agentic AI平台从0到1交付；此前三年在商业侧做数据中台、业务中台和商业数据平台的架构。2023-2025连续三年获Roche Valuable Contribution。我也写东西、办社区——一个两千多人的企业开源社区，和一个叫「碳硅之辨」的公众号系列，都是想把AI祛魅给不写代码的人。

The principal contradiction of the AI era is between people's surging anxiety about the technology and the scarcity of places to actually put it to work. I've spent 15 years closing that gap: from FP&A, to ERP rollouts across 13 countries on 4 continents, to data and business platform architecture, to pushing Agentic AI from prototype to production inside a hundred-thousand-person multinational pharma — and into industry-and-government work on clinical and real-world research and healthcare evidence. A finance guy who can't code, in my book, makes a poor government-affairs hand.

Now Digital & AI Solution Architect at Roche (China), leading the China R&D AI strategy and the 0-to-1 delivery of several Agentic AI platforms, after three years architecting data, business and commercial-data platforms on the commercial side. Roche Valuable Contribution three years running, 2023-2025. I also write and build community — a 2,200-plus-member enterprise open-source community, and a public essay series called the carbon-silicon question — both about demystifying AI for the people who don't write code.

✦ 通用vs垂类 general vs vertical ✦
让人人用上一个够好的通用agent，比给每个部门做一个垂类agent，有价值得多。
Putting one good general agent in everyone's hands beats building a vertical agent for every department.

大公司的AI转型大多在投垂类——给HR做一个、给sales做一个、给法务做一个，每个都“看起来很贴合业务”。这是在重复数字化时代犯过的错：把通用工具切成部门专属版，然后每个部门把自己的孤岛筑得更高。万维网当年也面临这个选择——给每家公司做一个内部BBS，还是给所有人造一个浏览器。后者赢了，因为它把可能性下沉到了每一个具体的人。

Most enterprise AI programs bet on verticals — one agent for HR, one for sales, one for legal, each "tailored to the business." It repeats the mistake of the digitization era: slice a general tool into department-specific versions, and every department builds its silo a little higher. The web faced the same fork — an internal BBS for each company, or one browser for everyone. The browser won, because it pushed the possibility down to every single person.

我发起的内部开源社区「硅基世界漫游指南」就是这么干的——把AI从写代码扩展到业务侧偷懒，如今2200+人，开始人传人。有人把每周几小时的台账压到几分钟，有人派agent替自己盯消息、建工单、做PPT、跑数据分析、做情报、监听会议、提取视频，甚至应付强制培训……没有人是为了“促进公司AI转型”才做这些，每个人只是想偷自己的懒——但合起来，旧组织赖以维系的信息不对称，就这么被一点点打破了。

The internal open-source community I founded — "The Silicon Hitchhiker's Guide" — does exactly this: carrying AI from coding into the business side's everyday drudgery. 2,200+ members now, spreading person to person. People squeezed hours of weekly bookkeeping into minutes, and set agents to watch their messages, file tickets, build decks, crunch data, gather intel, sit in on meetings, pull video transcripts — even click through the mandatory training … Nobody does it to "advance the company's AI transformation" — everyone just wants to dodge their own grunt work. But added up, the information asymmetry the old org runs on gets chipped away.
